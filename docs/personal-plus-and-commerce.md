# AAA Free / Personal+ / paid modules

Status: schema and UI source only; the private database and billing backend are not deployed. Registration remains disabled.

## Identity provider data contract
- Supabase Auth owns the UUID, verified email, identity/provider identifiers and authentication metadata. No duplicate raw OAuth payload, ID token, access token or refresh token in public tables or GitHub.
- Google basic OIDC scopes: openid, email, profile. Optional given/family name, locale and photo may be provided; profile_details stores only user-selected names/locale. No contacts, Gmail, phone, address, birthday or YouTube scopes. Do not assume email alone proves two identities are the same; require verified session and safe explicit linking.
- Spotify /me may expose stable account_id (preferred since May 2026), id, display_name, images, profile URL and conditional or deprecated email/country/product fields. Provider integration disabled; no raw Spotify claims persisted in custom tables.
- Potential music endpoints: user-top-read (artists/tracks, approximate 4 weeks, 6 months, 1 year), user-follow-read, user-library-read, playlist-read-private, playlist-read-collaborative, user-read-recently-played, user-read-currently-playing, user-read-playback-state. Availability differs by access tier, endpoint and scope. Not the complete listening history.
- Spotify Developer Policy III.13 limits analysis/profiling and III.14 prohibits ML/AI ingestion. Do not request music scopes or store provider listening data for Personal+ unless explicit permitted use is established with Spotify, relevant GDPR assessment completed, and access granted. Quota mode permits five authenticated users in development. Provider policy table defaults deny, with a database constraint disabling music import. An approved integration would need its own migration, minimal source-specific storage, revocation and deletion flow; do not use manual preferences as an automated import workaround. Spotify disconnect requires deletion of associated Spotify personal data.

## User data
Existing auth.users/auth.identities, aaa_access, aaa_profiles, onboarding, consent_history, aaa_artist_favorites, aaa_saved_events, aaa_privacy_requests plus aaa_profile_details and aaa_music_preferences (user-declared track/album/genre/venue/series only). No automatic Google profile enrichment yet; optional fields remain unused until user-facing confirmation flow. Favorites are not evidence of listening activity. Recommendation first version matches explicit artist-event IDs and future active events, displays reason, no inferred sensitive attributes. Other preferences are stored but not yet part of ranking.

## Product authorization
modules, plans, plan_modules, offers, orders, subscriptions, entitlements, payment_events. Free grants derived from enabled free plan and current onboarding, approved account and Experience permission; Personal+ also requires active personalization. Premium is disabled and unpriced. Future payments require server-side checkout, signature verification, idempotent transactional webhook processing, validated amount/currency/offer/user, entitlement creation/revocation, invoice retention, refunds and cancellation; never grant from redirect/browser. No card data. Commercial order records must follow tax/accounting retention and approved deletion process. Payment processing is not implemented.
aaa_my_modules exposes own effective availability only. Backend must enforce aaa_private.has_module on every protected module request; browser display is not security. Before production test access after withdrawal, revoked account, stale policy, paid order refund, expired subscription and mismatched ownership; ensure account deletion and data export include new tables. Confirm identity linking, policy gate and RLS isolation with synthetic users.
